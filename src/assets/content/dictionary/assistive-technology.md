---
title: Assistive Technology (AT)
definition: Assistive Technology, or AT, is an umbrella term for assistive products and their related systems and services. It can range from low-tech, such as a cane for walking, to high-tech, such as screen readers for navigating the web.
sources: 
- sourceurl: https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/assistive-technology
perspectives:
- meaning: technology that helps me use the web, such as captions for understanding videos or prefers-reduced-motion so I don't get distracted
  role: neurodivergent person
- meaning: products which help maintain or improve my functioning related to cognition, communication, hearing, mobility, self-care and vision, thus enabling their health, well-being, inclusion and participation
  role: human
